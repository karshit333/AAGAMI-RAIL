<product_requirements>
The AAGAMI RAIL – SIH 2025 AI Train Traffic Control Prototype aims to address Indian Railways' congestion, scheduling, and disaster handling. The application needs a professional, minimal UI.
Key requirements include:
1.  **Dataset & Routes**: Preloaded 5 routes (A-B, B-C, C-D, D-E, B-E) and 8-10 trains with ID, priority, planned times. Train IDs like TRN001-TRN010.
2.  **AI Optimization**: Backend  for priority-based conflict resolution, throughput maximization, and delay minimization. Outputs optimized schedule, KPIs, and human-readable explanations, including Before vs After ETA.
3.  **Map View**: Schematic map with stations A-E, routes, and animated trains (color-coded by priority). Rerouted paths are yellow, blocked routes dark black.
4.  **Disaster Management AI**: Backend  handles scenarios like Flooded Track, Equipment Failure, Weather, Emergency Train, Train Breakdown, Human Strike. AI resolves disruptions, provides explanations, and a What Changed? summary. Duration and Speed Reduction fields were explicitly removed from the disruption designer, with these values being automatically calculated.
5.  **Performance Dashboard**: KPI Cards (Total trains, Avg delay, Throughput %, On Time, Rerouted). Technical charts and metrics (no financial metrics) focusing on operational improvements.
6.  **About Section**: Project context, problem, solution, features, impact.
7.  **Enhancements**: Interactive Train Details, Smart Alerts & Notifications, Advanced Disaster Scenarios.
8.  **Comprehensive System Needs (Latest additions)**: Leverage operations research and AI for conflict-free, feasible schedules; maximize throughput; re-optimize rapidly under disruptions; support what-if simulation; user-friendly interface with clear recommendations, explanations, and override capabilities; integrate with existing systems (signaling, TMS, timetables) via secure APIs; include audit trails, performance dashboards, and KPIs. Enhanced train classification (Express, Local, Freight, Maintenance) and infrastructure constraints (line capacity, gradients) are also required.
</product_requirements>
