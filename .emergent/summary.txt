<key_technical_concepts>
-   **Backend**: FastAPI (Python), MongoDB with Motor (async driver), Pydantic (data models), JWT (auth), Passlib (security).
-   **Frontend**: React, CRACO, Tailwind CSS, Shadcn/UI (Radix UI, Lucide React), Axios, React Router DOM.
-   **AI Algorithms**: Interval Intersection (conflict detection), Breadth-First Search (route optimization), Weighted Sorting (priority scheduling), Heuristic Modeling (impact prediction), Greedy Algorithm (delay minimization), Statistical Modeling (performance analytics).
